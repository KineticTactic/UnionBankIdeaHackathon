# Union Bank — Product Terms & Offer Rules

## Savings Account — Interest & Balance Rules

- **Regular Savings:** 3.50% p.a. (daily balance method); min balance ₹1,000 (urban), ₹500 (rural/semi-urban)
- **Premium Savings (>₹1 lakh):** 4.00% p.a.
- **Zero Balance Savings (specific schemes):** 0% min balance; interest 3.00% p.a.
- Interest credited: Last working day of each quarter

**Non-Maintenance Charge:** ₹150/quarter (urban); ₹100/quarter (semi-urban); waived if avg quarterly balance maintained.

---

## Fixed Deposits — Key Terms

- Min deposit: ₹1,000; no maximum
- Tenure range: 7 days to 10 years
- **Standard rates (illustrative):**
  - 7–45 days: 4.00%
  - 46–179 days: 5.25%
  - 180–364 days: 6.00%
  - 1 year–2 years: 6.75%
  - 2 years–5 years: 6.50%
  - 5 years–10 years: 6.25%
- **Senior Citizen Benefit:** +0.50% above standard on all tenures
- **Premature withdrawal:** Penalty = 1.00% below contracted rate; min 7-day lock-in

**Retention Offer Cap:** RM can approve up to +0.25% above standard FD rate without branch head approval. Above +0.25% requires branch head sign-off.

---

## Credit Cards — Active Offer Rules

- **Cashback Card:** 2% unlimited cashback on online spends; 0.5% offline; no annual fee if 12+ transactions/year
- **Travel Card:** 5× reward points on airlines & hotels; annual fee ₹999 (waived on spends > ₹1.5L/year)
- **Co-branded Fuel Card:** 4% fuel surcharge waiver; 2× points on fuel; ₹499/year

**Important:** Credit card offers cannot be combined with existing promotional offers running on the same card. Cashback is credited within 30 days of statement close.

---

## Personal Loans

- Rate range: 10.50% – 18.00% p.a. (reducing balance)
- Min loan: ₹50,000; Max: ₹25,00,000
- Tenure: 12–60 months
- Pre-approval eligibility: Net monthly income > ₹25,000; credit score > 720; existing customer > 6 months
- **Pre-approved offer validity:** 30 days from offer generation date. Do NOT re-present if offer expired.

---

## Home Loans

- Rate range: 8.50% – 9.75% p.a. (floating, linked to repo rate)
- Min loan: ₹10,00,000
- LTV: Up to 80% (properties < ₹75L), up to 75% (₹75L–₹5Cr)
- Processing fee: 0.50% of loan amount (max ₹10,000); waived on pre-approved offers
- Pre-approval letter valid: 90 days

---

## Salary Accounts

- Zero minimum balance for salary accounts receiving consistent salary credits
- Salary credit gap > 2 months: account reverts to regular savings (min balance applicable)
- Benefits: Free NEFT/RTGS unlimited, 5 free ATM withdrawals/month (any bank), free demand drafts

---

## Regulatory & Compliance Constraints

- **KYC lapsed:** Cannot open new FD or upgrade account tier. Must complete KYC first.
- **PAN not linked:** FCNR/NRE offers not applicable.
- **Section 269SS:** Cash deposits/loans above ₹20,000 must be via banking channels; RM cannot override.
- **DND Registry:** SMS/call marketing suppressed for registered numbers regardless of internal consent flag.
- **IRDAI advisory:** Insurance product offers must include disclosure of charges; cannot promise returns on ULIP.
